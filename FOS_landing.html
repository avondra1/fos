Envelope Budgeting
Assign every dollar a job before you spend it.
+
Why it matters
Most budgeting fails because you react to spending instead of planning it. By the time you check your balance, the damage is done. Envelope budgeting flips this — you decide where money goes before you spend it. Every dollar has a job. Nothing drifts.
How it works in FOS
Create envelopes for each spending category — Rent, Groceries, Gas, Dining. When your paycheck arrives, use the Paycheck Allocator to fund each one. When you log a transaction, tag it to an envelope and its balance decreases in real time. When an envelope hits zero, you know exactly why.
💌
Paycheck Allocator
Split your paycheck across envelopes in one step.
+
Why it matters
Getting paid without a plan means money drifts toward whatever's in front of you. The allocator forces a deliberate moment every payday: this dollar goes here, not there. It's the difference between a budget you follow and one you forget about by Thursday.
How it works in FOS
Enter your take-home amount and select the account it landed in. FOS suggests splits based on your envelope monthly budgets. Adjust any amount. Watch the unallocated counter tick toward $0. Hit Apply — the deposit is logged and every envelope is funded in one step.

Net Worth Tracking
The one number that shows if you're actually building wealth.
+
Why it matters
Income and spending tell you the short story. Net worth tells you the long one. It's the only number that shows whether you're actually building wealth or just moving money around. A growing net worth means you're winning — regardless of how the month felt.
How it works in FOS
All your accounts — checking, savings, retirement, investment, debt — contribute to a live net worth total. Log a snapshot periodically and FOS builds a trend chart over time. Avalanche and snowball debt payoff projections show you the fastest path to zero.

Transaction Register
Every transaction written down. Reconcilable. Honest.
+
Why it matters
You can't manage what you don't track. A register is the source of truth — the way people balanced checkbooks before apps existed. It still works because it's honest: every transaction in, every transaction out, a running balance you can check against your bank statement.
How it works in FOS
Add transactions manually or import directly from a bank CSV. Running balance calculated automatically per account. Reconcile monthly against your statement — any difference gets flagged so you find missing transactions or post an adjustment. Bank CSV import auto-detects columns and skips duplicates.

Most people have no idea if they're on track. Now you will.
+
Why it matters

Recurring Templates
One-click logging for bills that happen every month.
+
Why it matters
Bills that repeat every month shouldn't require re-entering every month — but silently auto-adding them is worse, because you lose visibility. Templates give you the best of both: one-click speed with full awareness. You still log it. You just don't have to rebuild it from scratch every time.

Questions

Things people ask

Where exactly is my data stored?+
In your browser's local storage — a private area built into every modern browser. It never leaves your device. FOS has no server that receives your financial data. The only optional network request is for live stock prices, which requires an API key you control.
What if I clear my browser or switch computers?+
FOS reminds you to export a backup regularly. Your exported file is the source of truth — import it any time to fully restore everything on any browser, any computer. Think of it like a document: save the file and you're safe.
Do I need to install anything?+
No installation. FOS is a single HTML file that opens in any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge. Just open it and it works. No extensions, no accounts, no setup that requires a Google login.
How do I get my bank transactions in?+
Go to your bank's website and download transactions as a CSV — most banks call it "Export" or "Download transactions." Then in FOS, open Register → Import CSV. The importer detects your columns automatically and shows a preview before committing. Works with Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Ally, and most standard bank formats.
What is envelope budgeting?+
Envelope budgeting means assigning every dollar of income to a specific purpose before you spend it — Rent, Groceries, Car, Savings. When an envelope runs out, you stop spending in that category, or consciously move money from another. It's consistently the most effective budgeting method because it makes tradeoffs visible and forces intentional decisions.
What's coming and when?+
Encrypted cloud sync, automatic bank connections, and mobile support are on the roadmap. We won't ship the sync features until the encryption model is right — data will be encrypted on your device before it ever leaves. Sign up above and we'll let you know when things are ready.
Can I use this for my whole household?+
Yes. FOS supports multiple accounts and you can name everything however you like. For fully separate tracking between two people, use separate browser profiles — each has its own local storage. Household and family features are on the roadmap.
