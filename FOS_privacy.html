Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 29, 2026 · Last updated: March 29, 2026
The short version: FOS is designed so that we cannot read your financial data even if we wanted to. Your transactions, balances, and financial history live in your browser. They never reach our servers. This policy explains exactly what we do and don't collect.

1. Who we are

FOS ("Finance Operating System") is a personal finance application operated at getfos.app. When we say "FOS," "we," "us," or "our" in this policy, we mean the operators of getfos.app.

2. Your financial data — stored locally, not by us

FOS stores all of your financial data in your browser's local storage. This includes your transactions, accounts, envelopes, goals, net worth history, and all settings.

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers, not stored in any database we operate, and not accessible to us in any way. When you export a backup, the file goes directly to your device — it does not pass through our infrastructure.

Plain English: We literally cannot see your financial data. It lives on your computer, not ours. This is a technical guarantee, not just a policy promise.

3. What we do collect

The only personal information FOS collects is your email address, and only if you voluntarily provide it through our waitlist or notification signup on getfos.app.

When you sign up for our waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address — to notify you when new features launch
  • The date you signed up — for our own records

We do not collect your name, location, device information, financial data, or any behavioral analytics from the FOS application itself.

4. How we use your email

We use your email address for one purpose: to send you product updates when new features or versions of FOS are released. Specifically:

  • A one-time welcome email with a link to download FOS
  • Occasional product update emails when significant features ship

We will never sell your email address. We will never share it with advertisers. We will never send you marketing emails unrelated to FOS.

5. Analytics and tracking

The FOS application has no analytics. We do not track which screens you visit, how long you use the app, what features you use, or any behavioral data.

The getfos.app website (this marketing site) may use basic server-side access logs provided by our hosting provider (Cloudflare). These logs may include IP addresses and pages visited. We do not use third-party analytics services like Google Analytics on this website.

6. Optional error reporting

The FOS application includes an optional anonymous error reporting feature. If you opt in, error messages and screen names may be sent when the app encounters a bug. This feature:

  • Never sends financial data — no amounts, account names, or balances
  • Only sends the error message, the screen name where it occurred, and your browser type
  • Is opt-in — off by default, controlled in Settings
  • Can be disabled at any time in Settings → Privacy

7. Data retention

Your financial data: Stored in your browser's local storage. Retained until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app. We have no copy and cannot delete it on your behalf.

Your email address: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. To remove your email from our list, reply to any email we've sent you with "unsubscribe" or email us at hello@getfos.app.

8. Third-party services

FOS uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting and DNS. Subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Chart.js — loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com for charts within the app. No data is sent to Chart.js.
  • Google Fonts — loaded for typography. Google may log font requests per their privacy policy.
  • Alpha Vantage — optional, if you choose to enable live stock price updates in the Portfolio screen. Requires an API key you obtain and control. No financial data is sent — only ticker symbols for price lookups.

We do not use advertising networks, social media pixels, or data brokers.

9. Children's privacy

FOS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us their email address, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your rights

Regarding your email address, you have the right to:

  • Access — know what information we hold about you
  • Deletion — have your email removed from our list
  • Correction — update your email address
  • Portability — receive a copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@getfos.app. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Security

Because your financial data never leaves your device, the primary security responsibility rests with you — your device, your browser, and your browser profile. We recommend using a device with a strong password and keeping your browser up to date.

For the email list, we use standard industry practices to protect the small amount of personal data we hold.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify users on our email list. Continued use of FOS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about this policy?

Email us at hello@getfos.app and we'll respond within 2 business days. We're real people — you'll get a real answer.
