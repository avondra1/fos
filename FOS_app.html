Key concepts
Envelope budgeting — what it is and why it works ▾
Every dollar gets assigned a job before you spend it.

Envelope budgeting comes from the old practice of putting physical cash into labeled envelopes — one for rent, one for groceries, one for gas. When the envelope is empty, you stop spending in that category.

In FOS:

  • Each envelope represents one spending category or savings goal
  • When you get paid, you "fund" each envelope through the Paycheck Allocator
  • When you spend money, you tag the transaction to an envelope — the balance decreases
  • You always know exactly how much is left in each envelope
  • The "unallocated balance" in the Paycheck Allocator shows money sitting in your accounts without a job — should always be near $0

Why it works better than traditional budgeting:

  • You make spending decisions before you spend, not after
  • You can see immediately if a purchase will overdraw an envelope
  • Savings goals (house fund, vacation) get funded deliberately each paycheck
Book balance vs Cleared balance vs Uncleared ▾
Three different views of what's in your account.

Book balance

Every transaction you have entered in FOS, regardless of whether the bank has processed it yet. This is what you think you have based on everything you know.

Cleared balance

Only transactions you've marked with a ✓ checkmark — meaning they've appeared on your bank statement and you've confirmed them. This is what your bank says you have.

Uncleared

The difference — transactions you've entered but the bank hasn't posted yet. Could be a check in the mail, a pending debit card charge, or a transaction you entered early.

Which one to use day to day?

Use the book balance for spending decisions — it accounts for everything including what hasn't posted yet. Use the cleared balance when balancing against your bank statement.

Reconciliation — how to balance your checkbook ▾
Making sure your register matches your bank statement.
1
Get your bank statement (online or paper) showing all transactions that have posted.
2
In FOS, go to Accounts or Register and click Reconcile on the account.
3
Enter the ending balance from your bank statement in the "Bank statement balance" field.
4
Go through the list of uncleared transactions. Check off each one that appears on your bank statement.
5
When the difference reaches $0.00 — you're balanced. Click Done.
6
If there's still a difference after checking everything: either find the missing transaction, or post an Adjustment with a note (bank fee, interest, receipt you lost). The adjustment keeps your audit trail clean.

How often should I reconcile?

Once a month when you get your statement is enough. Some people do it weekly. The more often you do it, the easier it is because there are fewer transactions to compare.

Transaction accounts vs manual accounts ▾
How different account types work in FOS.

Transaction accounts (Checking, Savings, HYSA)

Balance is calculated automatically from every transaction you enter in the register. You never manually set the balance — it's always the sum of all your transactions.

Manual accounts (Retirement, HSA, Investment)

Balance is updated manually. These accounts change because of market performance (your 401k going up or down), not because of transactions you make. You update them periodically — weekly, monthly, whenever you check.

Debt accounts (Loan, Credit Card)

Balance is updated manually. Always shows as negative in net worth. Update the balance as you make payments. Example: your car loan starts at -$11,223. Each month after your payment, update it to the new lower balance.

Tip

After updating manual account balances, FOS will ask if you want to log your net worth — say yes to keep your trend chart current.

Rollover vs reset envelopes ▾
What happens to leftover money at month end.

Rollover

Unused balance carries forward to next month. Good for:

  • Savings goals (Christmas fund, vacation, house fund)
  • Irregular expenses (car maintenance, medical)
  • Any category where you're building up a buffer

Reset

Balance zeroes out at month end. Good for:

  • Fixed monthly expenses (rent, car payment, phone)
  • Categories where you want a fresh start each month
  • Discretionary spending (dining out, entertainment)

How it works in FOS

When you click "Close month" in the Register, reset envelopes automatically post a zeroing adjustment. Rollover envelopes carry their balance forward with no action needed.

Net worth — how it's calculated ▾
Assets minus debt, updated automatically.

Formula

Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Debt

What counts as assets

  • All transaction account balances (checking, savings, HYSA) — live from register
  • All manual account balances you've entered (retirement, HSA, investments)

What counts as debt

  • All Debt and Credit Card accounts — always negative
  • Example: car loan of $11,223 shows as -$11,223

Keeping it accurate

Transaction accounts are always accurate because every transaction updates them. Manual accounts are only as accurate as your last update — aim to update them once a month. Log a net worth entry each time you update to keep the trend chart meaningful.

Step-by-step workflows
I just got paid — what do I do? ▾
The paycheck workflow from start to finish.
1
Go to Paycheck Allocator in the sidebar.
2
Enter today's date and select the account your paycheck was deposited to.
3
Enter your net take-home pay (after taxes — what actually hit your account).
4
The app suggests splits based on your envelope monthly budgets. Review them — adjust any amounts. The "Unallocated" counter should reach $0.00.
5
Click Apply allocation. The app logs the paycheck deposit and creates allocation entries for each envelope.
6
Check the Register to confirm everything looks right.

Tips

  • If you get paid bi-weekly, run the allocator twice a month
  • The "unallocated balance" panel shows money sitting without a job — allocate it
  • You can adjust allocations freely — the suggested amounts are just a starting point
End of month — closing out ▾
What to do when a month is done.
1
Make sure all transactions for the month are entered in the Register.
2
Reconcile each transaction account against your bank statement (Register → Reconcile button on each account card).
3
Review the Reports screen for the month — check Budget vs Actual tab to see where you over or underspent.
4
Update manual account balances (Net Worth → Update manual accounts) with current retirement, HSA, and debt balances from your statements.
5
Log a net worth entry (Net Worth → + Log entry) to record this month's snapshot.
6
In Register, click Close month. Enter the month just ended. Confirm — this archives transactions and processes envelope rollovers/resets.
My register balance doesn't match my bank — what now? ▾
Troubleshooting the most common issue.

Most common reasons

  • Pending transactions — you entered something that hasn't posted yet, or the bank posted something you didn't enter
  • Missing transaction — a purchase, fee, or deposit you forgot to enter
  • Bank fee — monthly fee, ATM fee, overdraft fee not in the register
  • Interest income — HYSA interest posted to your account
  • Duplicate entry — you entered the same transaction twice

How to find it

1
Go to Register → click Reconcile on the account in question.
2
Enter your bank statement balance. The app shows the difference.
3
Check off each cleared transaction. The difference should shrink toward zero.
4
If you can't find it, post an Adjustment with a note explaining the difference. This keeps the register accurate without breaking anything.

FAQ
Is my data safe? ▾
Security and privacy explained.

Your data lives entirely in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to any server. FOS has no backend, no database, and no accounts. Your financial data never leaves your device.

The only thing you need to protect is your device and browser. Your data is not accessible to anyone unless they have access to your computer and browser profile.

Most important step: export a backup regularly (Settings → Export data). If you clear your browser data, local storage is wiped — your exported JSON backup is how you restore everything.

What's the difference between a category and an envelope? ▾
They sound similar but serve different purposes.

Category — a label describing what a transaction is. Used for reporting and analysis. Examples: Groceries, Dining Out, Gas, Paycheck. You can have many categories and they're just for organization.

Envelope — a budget bucket linked to an account with a monthly amount. Has a running balance. Examples: Food ($300/mo), Rent ($1,500/mo), House Fund. Envelopes are funded by your paycheck and depleted by spending.

When you add a transaction, you pick both. Category = what it was. Envelope = which budget bucket it comes from. Often they match (category "Rent" → envelope "Rent") but not always (category "Groceries" → envelope "Food").

Can I use this on my phone? ▾
Mobile access options.

FOS is designed primarily for desktop use — open the HTML file in any modern browser on your computer and it works immediately, no installation needed.

For on-the-go tracking, the best workflow is: keep a note on your phone (Notes app, Google Keep, or even a text message to yourself) of purchases during the day, then enter them in FOS when you get home. Many people find this works better anyway — it forces you to review each transaction intentionally.

Mobile support and cross-device sync are on the roadmap for a future version.

What if I need to edit or delete a transaction? ▾
Fixing mistakes in the register.

Edit: click any row in the Register. The Add Transaction drawer opens pre-filled with that transaction's details. Change what you need and click Update.

Delete: click the red ✕ button on the right side of any row. You'll be asked to confirm. Deleting a transaction removes it from the running balance and the linked envelope.

Tip: if you accidentally delete something, you can re-enter it. If you've already closed that month, you can view the archive in localStorage (browser developer tools → Application → Local Storage → look for fos_archive_MM_YYYY).

What if I get a new version of the app? ▾
Updating without losing your data.

Your data lives in your browser's localStorage. Replacing the HTML file has zero effect on your data — it's stored separately in the browser, not in the file itself.

1
Before updating: export a backup from Settings → Export data. This is your safety net.
2
Download the new FOS.html file.
3
Replace your old file with the new one (same browser, same folder).
4
Open it in your browser — your data loads automatically from localStorage.

If something looks off after updating, use Settings → Import data to restore from your backup.

How do I import my ClearCheckbook history? ▾
Migrating your transaction history.
1
In ClearCheckbook: go to Import/Export → Export Transactions → CSV.
2
Open the CSV in Excel or a spreadsheet app to review the columns.
3
Match columns to FOS format: Date (MM/DD/YYYY), Description, Category, EnvelopeID (leave blank), AccountID (your account's ID from FOS), Type (Withdrawal/Deposit), Amount (negative for withdrawals), blank, Cleared (TRUE/FALSE), Memo, blank, ID (leave blank).
4
In FOS, go to Register → Import CSV. Select your formatted CSV file. FOS will auto-detect columns and show a preview before importing.

Note: account IDs in FOS are generated codes like "a1234567890". You'll need to update the AccountID column in your import with the correct IDs from your FOS accounts.

How do I back up and restore my data? ▾
Keeping your data safe and recovering from browser clears.

Exporting a backup

1
Go to Settings → scroll to the Data section.
2
Click "Export data" — this downloads a .json file with all your transactions, accounts, envelopes, goals, and settings.
3
Save this file somewhere safe — your Downloads folder, a USB drive, or a cloud storage service.

Restoring from a backup

1
Go to Settings → click "Import data".
2
Select your .json backup file. The app will show you what's in it before replacing anything.
3
Confirm — your data is restored exactly as it was when you exported.

How often should I export?

Once a month is a good habit — FOS will remind you if it's been more than 30 days. Export before any browser updates or if you're switching computers.
